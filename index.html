SPARR Organize

MORE THAN EVENT
MANAGEMENT.

Smoothcomp handles brackets. SPARR Organize is an event community — combining logistics, real-time communication, fan interaction and social layer in one platform.

Event Creation

Tournaments, fight nights, seminars, sparring days, camps.

Registration

Athletes, coaches, teams, staff. Weight, age, level, discipline.

Brackets & Schedule

Auto or manual matchmaking. Mat, ring and hall planning.

Fan Interaction

Follow athletes, live updates, fight reminders, highlights.

Tickets & Check-in

Spectator tickets, QR code check-in, access control.

Sponsor Tools

Sponsor pages, banners, giveaways, visibility packages.

Staff Management

Helpers, referees, security, check-in team — all connected.

Content & Feed

Event feed, clips, winner posts, stories, live coverage.
