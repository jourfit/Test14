The Problem

COMBAT ATHLETES TRAIN HARD — BUT THEIR PROGRESS DISAPPEARS.

Most fighters train across gyms, roadwork, sparring, strength sessions and fight camps. But progress is scattered across notes, chats, memories and random social posts. SPARR brings it together.

📝

Training gets forgotten

Sessions disappear into memory. No log, no pattern, no visible progress.

🥊

Sparring has no structure

Great rounds happen. Nobody tracks the rounds, the intensity, the learning.

🏕️

Fight camps are hard to track

Weeks of work go unlogged. The journey exists but leaves no trail.

💬

Gym communities live in messy chats

WhatsApp groups, loose Instagram posts. No central community layer.

📱

Athletes post highlights, not progress

Instagram shows the knockout. Not the 200 rounds that led to it.
